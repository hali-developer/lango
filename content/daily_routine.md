# Daily Routine

## Vocabulary
- **Wake up**: To stop sleeping.
- **Breakfast**: The first meal of the day.
- **Commute**: To travel some distance between one's home and place of work on a regular basis.
- **Exercise**: Physical activity done to maintain health and fitness.
- **Unwind**: To relax after a period of work or tension.

## Grammar Focus: Present Simple for Habits
We use the Present Simple tense to talk about habits, routines, and things that are generally true.
- *I wake up at 7:00 AM every day.*
- *She commutes to work by train.*
