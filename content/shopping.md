# Shopping

## Vocabulary
- **Receipt**: A written or printed statement acknowledging that something has been paid for.
- **Discount**: A deduction from the usual cost of something.
- **Refund**: A repayment of a sum of money.
- **Bargain**: A thing bought or offered for sale more cheaply than is usual or expected.
- **Cashier**: A person handling payments and receipts in a store.

## Grammar Focus: Quantifiers (Some, Any, Much, Many)
We use quantifiers to talk about the amount of something.
- *Do you have any discounts available?*
- *I bought some apples.*
- *How much does this cost?*

## Sentence Making Prompt
Describe a recent shopping experience using the vocabulary word **Bargain** and a **quantifier** (e.g., some, any, much, many).

## Quiz
1. What do you call the paper that proves you paid for an item?
2. Write a sentence using the word "Refund".
