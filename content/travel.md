# Travel

## Vocabulary
- **Airport**: The place you go to catch a flight.
- **Luggage**: The bags you take with you on a trip.
- **Passport**: An official document required for international travel.
- **Reservation**: An arrangement to have a room, seat, etc., held for your use.
- **Itinerary**: A planned route or journey.

## Grammar Focus: Present Continuous for Future Arrangements
We often use the Present Continuous tense to talk about fixed arrangements in the future.
- *I am flying to Paris tomorrow.*
- *We are staying at the Hilton Hotel.*
