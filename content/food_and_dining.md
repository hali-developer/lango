# Food & Dining

## Vocabulary
- **Appetizer**: A small dish of food or a drink taken before a meal.
- **Beverage**: A drink, especially one other than water.
- **Delicious**: Highly pleasant to the taste.
- **Menu**: A list of dishes available in a restaurant.
- **Tip**: A small sum of money given to someone for a service.

## Grammar Focus: Modals of Polite Request (Would like, Could, May)
We use modals like "would like" or "could" to make polite requests, especially in restaurants.
- *I would like the steak, please.*
- *Could we have the bill?*
- *May I see the menu?*

## Sentence Making Prompt
Politely order a meal at a restaurant using the vocabulary word **Beverage** and a **modal of polite request**.
