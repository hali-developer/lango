# Work & Business

## Vocabulary
- **Meeting**: An assembly of people for a particular purpose, especially for formal discussion.
- **Deadline**: The latest time or date by which something should be completed.
- **Colleague**: A person with whom one works in a profession or business.
- **Promotion**: The action of raising someone to a higher position or rank.
- **Schedule**: A plan for carrying out a process or procedure.

## Grammar Focus: Present Perfect for Recent Actions
We use the Present Perfect tense to talk about actions that happened at an unspecified time before now, often with relevance to the present.
- *I have finished the report.*
- *She has scheduled a meeting for tomorrow.*
